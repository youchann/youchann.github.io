Privacy Policy

Maitsuki (iOS app)

日本語 · Support

Summary

  • No account is required. No personal data is collected.
  • The developer operates no servers. Your app data stays on your device.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Location access is never requested.
  • What you record — which services you pay for and how much — never reaches the developer.

This policy applies to “Maitsuki”, an iOS app provided by independent developer Yutaro Hayashi, and to this website.

1. Information we do not collect

Maitsuki requires no account, and every feature works without signing up. The subscriptions you record (name, amount, billing cycle, billing dates, category, status, and icon image) and your settings are all stored on your device.

The developer operates no servers, so there is no mechanism by which the app could send personal data anywhere. Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, and device identifiers are never collected. The app does not connect to bank accounts, credit cards, or your accounts with any service. Every amount and billing date is something you entered yourself.

To help investigate problems, when an operation such as saving or scheduling a reminder fails, the app records the kind of operation and the error number in the device's system log. Subscription names and amounts are not included, and the app never sends this record anywhere.

2. Notifications

Reminders before a billing date or the end of a trial are scheduled and shown on your device. Notification permission is used solely to show these reminders on your device. There is no server that sends notifications, and no device identifier for notifications is obtained.

3. Photos

When you set an image as a subscription's icon, the app uses the iOS photo picker. Only the one image you choose is handed to the app; access to your whole photo library is never requested. The chosen image is reduced in size, stored on your device, and never sent anywhere.

4. Exporting and importing data

The file created by Export Data contains the subscriptions you recorded and the icon images you set. The file goes only to the destination you choose to save or share it to, and is not sent to the developer. The file is not encrypted, so please take care over who you share it with and where you keep it.

5. Tracking and advertising

No analytics SDK, A/B testing platform, advertising identifier (IDFA), or ad network is integrated. The app shows no ads. It uses no third-party SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Location

Location access is never requested, obtained, or used.

7. Purchase information

Pro (the lifetime purchase and the monthly subscription) is bought through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your credit card number, address, Apple Account, or any other payment information. Purchase status is verified on your device through Apple's StoreKit. This is the only network communication the app performs.

8. Information Apple provides to the developer

Through App Store Connect, Apple provides the developer with statistics such as download counts, sales, and crash reports. These are aggregated and do not identify individuals, and some of them — crash reports, for example — are provided only if you have enabled “Share With App Developers” in iOS Settings. The developer has no other means of learning how the app is used.

9. If you contact us

If you email support, the developer receives your message and the sending email address. An email started from Contact Us in the app has the app version, iOS version, and device model already filled in (you can see them before sending and remove them). Email is received via Gmail (Google LLC).

What you send is used only to answer your inquiry and to address the issue afterwards. It is never used for marketing or provided to third parties, and it is deleted once it is no longer needed. If you would like it deleted sooner, just let us know.

10. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. Since no personal data is collected at all, no personal data is collected from children either.

11. Deleting your data and your rights

Your app data lives on your device and the developer holds none of it, so deletion is entirely in your hands.

  • Subscriptions you recorded can be deleted individually in the app
  • Deleting the app also deletes the data and settings stored on your device
  • Notification permission can be revoked at any time from iOS Settings
  • If you use device backups (such as iCloud Backup), your app data is included in those backups by Apple's own mechanism. Backups are managed from iOS Settings

Because the developer holds no account for you, there is nothing to cancel and no data request to file. The only exception is an inquiry email you have already sent — contact us at the address below if you would like it deleted.

12. About this website

This page is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may record information such as IP addresses when the page is served. The developer does not receive those access logs. This site uses no analytics and no tracking cookies.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page along with the date of the update. The version shown here is always the current one.
