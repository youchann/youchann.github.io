Maitsuki Support

Maitsuki (“every month” in Japanese) is an iPhone app that keeps track of your subscriptions in one place. It shows how much you pay each month and when the next charge is due.

FAQ

I'm not getting reminders

Please check the following, in order.

  • Notifications are allowed for Maitsuki under Settings → Notifications → Maitsuki on your iPhone
  • “Remind before billing” is turned on in that subscription's edit screen
  • The day and time are what you expect (by default, 9:00 AM one day before the billing date; the time can be changed in the app's Settings)

Subscriptions marked as canceled do not send reminders. iOS also limits how many notifications an app can schedule, so Maitsuki schedules the nearest ones first and reschedules whenever you open the app. If you have not opened the app for a long time, later reminders may not arrive, so please open it now and then.

Does “Mark as Canceled” cancel the service itself?

No. Maitsuki only records and displays your payments; it is not connected to any service. To cancel a service, follow that service's own procedure. “Mark as Canceled” simply records in the app that you have canceled it.

The amount or billing date differs from what I am actually charged

The prices filled in from a template are the prices for Japan that the developer confirmed at the time, and they are a guide only. They can differ from what you pay because of price changes, promotions, or how you signed up. You can correct the amount, billing cycle, and first billing date at any time in the edit screen. Prices are filled in only when the app's currency is Japanese yen.

The next billing date is calculated from the first billing date and the billing cycle. For a monthly subscription that started on the 31st, months without a 31st use their last day.

How many subscriptions can I add for free?

Up to 10. Active subscriptions and those on a free trial count; canceled ones do not. Pro removes the limit. The limit is the only thing Pro changes: the calendar, analytics, reminders, dark mode, custom icon images, and data export and import are all free.

How much does it cost?

Pro is available as a one-time lifetime purchase or as a monthly subscription. Both unlock exactly the same thing. For current prices, see the purchase screen in the app or the App Store product page.

I want to cancel the monthly Pro subscription

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

If you have more than 10 subscriptions after canceling, none of them are removed. You can still view and edit them as before. Only adding new ones is unavailable until you are at 10 or fewer.

I want to restore my purchase

Use Restore Purchases in the app's Settings. As long as you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with, you do not need to buy again. The same applies after switching devices or reinstalling.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. The developer is not able to process refunds.

I want to move my data to a new iPhone

Save a file with Settings → Export Data in the app, then choose that file with Settings → Import Data on the new device. When importing, you can choose whether to replace what is there or add to it.

Maitsuki has no iCloud sync. Your data lives only on your device, so deleting the app deletes the data too. Exporting before you delete the app is recommended.

If I change the currency, are amounts converted?

No. The app uses a single currency, and changing it in Settings leaves the numbers as they are (¥1,000 becomes $1,000). For a service billed in another currency, enter the amount in your own currency.

Where is my data stored?

Your subscriptions and settings are stored only on your device. No account is required, and nothing is sent to a server. There are no ads and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy

Maitsuki requires no account and collects no personal data. The developer operates no servers and has integrated no analytics or advertising. What you record — which services you pay for and how much — never reaches the developer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
