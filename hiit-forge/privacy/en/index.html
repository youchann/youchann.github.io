Privacy Policy

HIIT Forge (iOS / Apple Watch app)

日本語 · Support

At a glance

  • No account is required. No personal data is collected.
  • The developer runs no servers. Your data stays on your device.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Location is never requested.
  • Health (HealthKit) access is optional, every feature works without it, and health data is never sent to the developer.

This policy applies to HIIT Forge, an iOS and Apple Watch app published by Yutaro Hayashi, an independent developer, and to this website.

1. What is not collected

HIIT Forge requires no account, and every feature works without signing up. The menus you build, your workout history, and your settings are stored on your device.

The developer operates no servers, so there is no mechanism by which the app could send personal data anywhere. The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or any device identifier.

2. Tracking and advertising

The app contains no analytics SDK, no A/B testing framework, no advertising identifier (IDFA), and no ad networks. It shows no ads. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Location

The app never asks for location permission, and never collects or uses location data.

4. Health (HealthKit)

Health integration is entirely optional. If you decline it, every feature of the app still works.

If you grant permission, the app uses Health data as follows:

  • During a workout, it reads heart rate and active energy from your Apple Watch and displays them on screen
  • When a workout ends, it writes that workout to the Health app

This data stays on your device and in your own Health database. It is never transmitted to the developer, never used for advertising, and never sold or shared with third parties. You can revoke permission at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Purchases

All paid features are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your credit card number, billing address, or Apple ID. Purchase status is verified on your device.

6. Information Apple provides to the developer

Through App Store Connect, Apple provides developers with statistics such as download counts, sales, and crash reports. This information is aggregated and does not identify individuals, and some of it — crash reports in particular — is provided only when you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings. The developer has no other way of knowing how the app is used.

7. If you contact support

When you email support, the developer receives what you write and the email address you send it from. Email is received through Gmail (Google LLC).

Your message is used only to answer your question and to work on any problem you reported. It is not used for marketing and is not shared with third parties. Messages are deleted once they are no longer needed. If you would like yours deleted sooner, just ask.

8. Optional diagnostic log

When reporting a problem, you may optionally attach a diagnostic log. It is never sent automatically — sending it is always your choice. The log contains the following:

Included

  • App version, iOS / watchOS version
  • Device model, device language setting
  • Timestamps, success or failure, and error codes for workout runs and Apple Watch sync events
  • The number of saved menus and their internal identifiers

Not included

  • Your name, email address, or account information
  • Device identifiers or location data
  • Health data (heart rate, calories, or any other values)
  • Text you have typed, such as menu names
  • Data from other apps, such as photos or contacts

9. Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. Because no personal data is collected at all, no personal data is knowingly collected from children.

10. Deleting your data, and your rights

Your data lives on your device and the developer does not hold it, so deletion is entirely in your hands.

  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device
  • Menus and history can be deleted individually inside the app
  • Workouts written to Health can be deleted in the Health app

There is no account held by the developer, so there is nothing to close and no data access request to file. The one exception is support email you have already sent — contact the address below if you want it deleted.

11. About this website

This page is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may record information such as IP addresses as part of serving it; the developer does not receive those access logs. This site uses no analytics and no tracking cookies.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page along with the date it was updated. The version on this page is always the current one.
