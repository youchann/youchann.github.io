Frequently asked questions

I want to restore my purchase

Use "Restore Purchases" in the app's settings screen. As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with, you do not need to buy it again. The same applies after switching devices or reinstalling.

I want to cancel my subscription

Go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The app's settings screen also has a direct link to that page. Cancellation is handled entirely by Apple; the developer cannot do it on your behalf.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. The developer is not able to issue refunds.

How does pricing work?

Pro is available as a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase. There is no annual plan. Without paying anything you still get the full timer, CG animations for the basic exercises, the built-in preset workouts, and running workouts on Apple Watch. For current prices, see the app's App Store product page.

My workouts aren't syncing to Apple Watch

Check these in order:

  • Your iPhone and Apple Watch are paired and signed in to the same Apple ID
  • You opened the app on iPhone, then opened it on the Apple Watch as well
  • The sync status shown in the app (last synced time) is updating

If that doesn't resolve it, send the details above and, if you like, a diagnostic log.

My workouts aren't showing up in the Health app

Writing to Health requires permission. Check HIIT Forge under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, or in the Health app under Sharing. Note that every feature of the app still works if you leave this off.

There's no sound, or my music keeps cutting out

Check your device's silent switch and volume. In the app's settings you can turn spoken exercise names, sound effects, and the countdown on or off independently, and change how audio mixes with your music app.

When running a workout on Apple Watch alone, spoken cues play only while Bluetooth headphones are connected. They do not play through the Watch's built-in speaker, which would drain the battery considerably. Without headphones, wrist haptics and the on-screen display still tell you what's coming next.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. The developer runs no servers, so your menus and history are never sent anywhere. See the privacy policy for details.

I want to report a bug

Email the steps to reproduce it along with your device details. You can optionally attach a diagnostic log. Exactly what that log does and does not contain is listed in the privacy policy.

Privacy

HIIT Forge requires no account and collects no personal data. The developer runs no servers, and the app contains no analytics and no advertising. See the privacy policy for the full details.
