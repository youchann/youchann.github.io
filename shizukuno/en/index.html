Shizukuno

A YouTube channel of seasonal scenes and music from Shizukuno (雫野), a quiet fictional town where rain and snow fall.

日本語 · Privacy Policy

Channel

youtube.com/@Shizukuno

The channel posts long-form background music, two to three hours per video, to keep you company while you work, study, read, or rest. Each video layers calm music and the sound of rain or snow over a scene from the town of Shizukuno.

Made with AI

All music, ambient sounds and background art in the videos are generated with AI. None of it imitates a real artist or song. The tools used are listed in each video's description.

About the upload tool

Videos are uploaded with "Shizukuno Uploader", a tool that the channel operator runs only on their own computer. Through YouTube API Services, it uploads videos to the Shizukuno channel and sets their thumbnails. It is not distributed or offered to anyone else.

The information the tool handles is described in the Privacy Policy.
